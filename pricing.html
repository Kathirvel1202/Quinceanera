PAYMENT QUESTIONS

Know what to ask before booking

What affects the final quote?
Service scope, guest count, event location, schedule, and the number of planning meetings can affect the quote.
Can we begin with one service?
Yes. Ask about a focused planning or design session if you need help with one area first.
When are payment terms shared?
Payment dates and any retainer are listed in the written proposal before booking.
