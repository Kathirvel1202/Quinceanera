COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you should know about booking & payments

Have questions about quotes, retainer schedules, or guest counts? Find clear answers below.

How does the deposit and payment schedule work?
To reserve your celebration date on our master calendar, we require a 25% date-lock retainer upon contract signing. The remaining balance is split into three equal installments: 25% at mid-planning, 25% at 60 days before the event, and the final 25% due 7 days prior to celebration day. We accept bank transfers, ACH, and all major credit cards with zero surcharge.
What factors can cause package starting prices to vary?
Starting rates cover typical single-venue celebrations within our regional service zone. Factors that may adjust pricing include: guest counts exceeding 250 (which require additional assistant coordinators for guest safety), multi-venue logistics (separate church, photo location, and reception ballroom), and holiday weekend dates (New Year's Eve, Memorial Day, Labor Day). All potential adjustments are disclosed transparently before you sign.
Can we begin with Day-Of Coordination and upgrade later?
Yes, absolutely! Many families begin with our Day-Of Coordination package to lock their date, and later decide they want our help with theme design, florist sourcing, or court rehearsals. 100% of your initial investment transfers toward Partial or Full-Service planning at any point during your planning cycle.
Do you take vendor commissions or mark up vendor pricing?
Never. We operate on a strict 100% fiduciary model. Any trade discounts or negotiated savings we secure with ballrooms, caterers, photographers, and DJs pass directly to your family. You pay vendors directly with zero hidden markups from our agency.
What is your policy if our party needs to be rescheduled?
Family milestones are sacred, and life happens. If illness, venue cancellation, or unforeseen family emergencies require moving your celebration date, 100% of your paid retainer transfers to any available open date within 12 months with zero penalty or administrative fees.
What happens if our event runs past the contracted hours on celebration day?
If the party is electric and your family chooses to extend the ballroom rental, overtime for our lead planner and coordination team is billed at a flat rate of $150 per hour. Overtime is only initiated upon verbal confirmation from the parents.
