GETTING STARTED

First decisions and timing

When should we begin planning?
Begin when you are ready to discuss a date range, guest estimate, and budget priorities. Earlier planning can give you more options, but the right timeline depends on the event.
What information should we prepare?
A date or date range, approximate guest count, preferred location, and a few must-have details are a helpful start.
Can we update decisions later?
Plans can evolve. Keep signed vendor agreements, deadlines, and availability in mind when changing details.

SERVICES

Choosing the kind of support

Do we need full-service planning?
Not necessarily. Choose the level of help based on what is already arranged and where your family would value guidance.
Can we ask for help with just décor or the timeline?
Ask about focused support for design direction, schedule organization, vendor questions, or another specific planning need.
Do you coordinate with our chosen vendors?
Coordination scope can be discussed during your consultation and included in the written proposal.

BUDGET & BOOKING

Understand scope before you commit

How is a planning quote prepared?
Quotes reflect the requested services, event size, schedule, location, and planning timeline. You will be able to review the scope before confirming.
Are package prices fixed?
Package descriptions are starting points. A written quote clarifies the specific services and fees for your event.
What happens after we book?
You receive the agreed next steps, planning milestones, and the details needed to begin the work.

GUESTS & TRADITIONS

Make the celebration welcoming and meaningful

Family traditions

Share traditions, important roles, and cultural details early so they can be included in the schedule and event plan.

Guest comfort

Consider access, seating, children, travel, and any accommodations guests may need.

Meaningful roles

Ask relatives and friends which parts they would enjoy helping with, then make each role clear in the schedule.

Food for everyone

Collect dietary needs early and check that menu choices, serving plans, and timing work for your guests.

Comfortable arrival

Share parking, entrance, accessibility, and transportation details before guests set out.

Capture family moments

Plan portraits and time with loved ones without letting photographs interrupt the whole celebration.
